> **Status:** In progress  
> **Partners:** PolyU Language Science and Technology; South China Normal University

A multimodal corpus of **dialogue**, **gesture**, **gaze**, **social interaction**, and concurrent **fNIRS**, collected with South China Normal University. Social interaction is coded as social state and expression skill. The corpus supports research on instructional gesture and embodied pedagogical agents.

Further technical details are not listed here while the work is ongoing.
