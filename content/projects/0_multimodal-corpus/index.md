> **Status:** In progress  
> **Partners:** PolyU Language Science and Technology; South China Normal University

A multimodal corpus of coordinated speech, gesture, and gaze from dyadic dialogue, collected with South China Normal University, together with concurrent fNIRS. The corpus supports research on instructional gesture and embodied pedagogical agents.
